# Third-party notices

Everything in this repository that isn't listed below was made for Borrowed Seconds. That covers
the code, the levels, the shaders, the Blender models and renders (generated by
`ArtSource/*.py`), and every sound effect and music loop (synthesised by `Tools/audio/synth.py`).
No stock art, samples or music are used.
